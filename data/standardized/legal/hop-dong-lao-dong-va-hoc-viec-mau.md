1. HOP DONG HOC VIEC VA THUC TAP TRONG DOANH NGHIEP (DIEU 61, 62 BO LUAT LAO DONG 2019)
- Hop dong dao tao/hoc viec phai ky bang van ban neu thoi gian hoc viec tu 01 thang tro len.
- Muc luong trong thoi gian hoc viec do hai ben thoa thuan. Truong hop nguoi hoc viec truc tiep lam ra san pham hoac thuc hien cong viec kinh doanh cho doanh nghiep thi phai duoc tra luong theo muc hai ben thoa thuan khong thap hon muc luong toi thieu vung.
